---
title: Adding New Documentation Pages
description: How to add and publish new docs for your project.
---

## Step-by-step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Project">
    ````
    Log in and select your desired project from the dashboard.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Page">
    ````
    Click "Add Page" or "+ New Doc" in the project sidebar.
    Enter a page title and content in the editor.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    ````
    Click "Publish" to make your new page live.
    Preview it to ensure everything looks correct.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
